# Values that appear only on plots (not digitized)

The Yau 2024 manuscript has no numerical results table. Plot points were not read off the figures.

- Figure 8 (p. 8): I–V curves (dark vs 5 mW) and angular photocurrent. Individual current values were not digitized. The caption states the 5 mW condition; section 4.1 states a maximum at 150 degrees.
- Figures 10 and 12: polar plots. Panel titles and some angle annotations are printed on the figures but were not copied into an analysis table, because they are not a typeset table and some labels are visually ambiguous.
- Figures 11 and 13: polarization angle vs concentration, with fitted lines. The text states a slope of 1.59 deg/(mg/dL) for Figure 11 and a good linear relationship for Figure 13, with no R-squared. Point coordinates were not digitized.
- Figures 14–19: machine-learning fit plots and learning curves. Loss values and iteration counts that appear in the text are in yau-2024-stated-results.csv. Other curve values were not digitized.
- Figure 21: printed 135 and 235 degree labels are in yau-2024-figure21-printed-angle-labels.csv. Photocurrent markers were not digitized.
